# Credits & data sources

**SkyEmpire is not affiliated with any airline or aircraft manufacturer. Names are used for identification only.**
Airline logos are **not** distributed with the game. Players can choose to download them from Wikimedia Commons for personal use.

| Data | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| Airports, runways | [OurAirports](https://ourairports.com/data/) | Public domain |
| City populations (airport catchment) | [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) cities15000 | CC BY 4.0 |
| GDP per capita (airport wealth) | [World Bank](https://data.worldbank.org/) NY.GDP.PCAP.CD | CC BY 4.0 |
| Country shapes (map) | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:50m | Public domain |
| Airline IDs, codes, countries | [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) | CC0 |
| Airline list, real routes (2014 snapshot) | [OpenFlights](https://openflights.org/data) | ODbL 1.0 |
| Aircraft specifications | Manufacturers' public specification pages (Airbus, Boeing, ATR, Embraer, De Havilland Canada, Bombardier/MHI RJ, COMAC), Wikidata | Facts; cited per aircraft in `packages/data/src/aircraft.json` |
| Live weather forecasts | [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (weather data by Open-Meteo.com) | CC BY 4.0 |
| Public holidays, festival dates | [date-holidays](https://github.com/commenthol/date-holidays) (used at build time) | ISC |
| Aircraft photos | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) — one per type, resized to WebP; credits below | CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 / public domain (per photo) |
| Aircraft real-world specs (length, span, height, engines, first flight, typical seating) | [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/), linked per aircraft in `packages/data/src/aircraft-details.json` | Facts |
| Aircraft silhouettes | Drawn by SkyEmpire from published dimensions | MIT (this project) |
| Fuel burn, lease, maintenance and crew costs | SkyEmpire estimates from published industry figures | — |
| Brand colors | Approximations chosen by SkyEmpire | — |

Airport attributes such as tourism, business, slot capacity and fees are generated by heuristics in `packages/data/src/heuristics.ts` and hand-tuned in `overrides.world.json`.

**Trailer** (`docs/media/skyempire-trailer.mp4`): music “Uplifting Corporate” by Soundrider/Dope ([Jamendo](https://www.jamendo.com/track/1670597), CC BY); sound effects from Openverse (CC0 / CC BY).
